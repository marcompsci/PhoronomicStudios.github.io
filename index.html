02 / WHAT I’M WORKING TOWARDS

Software that meets people
where they actually are.

My work begins with a simple premise: products should make a person’s own motion — their habits, goals, learning, health, and daily life — easier to see and act on.

I’m especially interested in AI systems that remain legible and accountable: every meaningful change planned, reviewed, checkpointed, and validated by a human.

Accessibility isn’t a later release. Voice-first interaction, keyboard support, reduced motion, clear feedback, and privacy are build constraints from the start.
