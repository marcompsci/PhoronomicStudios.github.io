03 / What I’m working towards

Software that meets people where they actually are.

I build tools that help people see and act on their own motion — their habits, goals, learning, health, and daily life.

The field

I’m a former Geek Squad Home Service Agent and Apple Product Specialist. I moved from Fresno to San Francisco to pursue a life in tech, build my own work, and create a better future for myself.

The focus

At Phoronomic Studios, I’m especially interested in AI systems that remain legible and accountable — software that explains itself instead of asking for blind trust.

The constraint

Accessibility isn’t a later release. Voice-first interaction, keyboard support, reduced motion, clear feedback, and privacy are build constraints from the start.

Questions

Things people ask first.

What actually happens in an AI workshop?

You fill in a short intake questionnaire first, so the day is built on your real work instead of a generic deck. Then it’s hands-on: live exercises on non-sensitive sample data, written security boundaries, and role-based templates you keep. Everyone leaves with an action plan and a scorecard we check again at thirty days.

Are the coaching prices per session or per month?

Per month. Each tier is a program: a set number of sessions, a written plan, and me on the other end of the phone in between.

Do you come to me, or do I come to you?

I come to you. Coaching happens at your park, field or gym anywhere across the Bay Area. Tech work is in your home. Web and content work can be fully remote.

What size TV can you mount?

65 inches and under, for now. Above that I’d need a second pair of hands, and I’d rather tell you that up front than drop your television.

Why is there no price on content creation?

Scope drives that number more than anything else, so a figure on a page would be a guess dressed up as a quote. Tell me the deliverable and you’ll get a real number the same day.

How fast do you answer?

Same day, most days. There’s no queue and no dispatcher, so the person reading your message is the person who shows up.
