02 / What I’m working towards

Software that meets people
where they actually are.

OriginI’m a former Geek Squad Home Service Agent and Apple Product Specialist. I moved from Fresno to San Francisco to pursue a life in tech, build my own work, and create a better future for myself.

FocusAt Phoronomic Studios, I build tools that help people see and act on their own motion — their habits, goals, learning, health, and daily life. I’m especially interested in AI systems that remain legible and accountable.

ConstraintAccessibility isn’t a later release. Voice-first interaction, keyboard support, reduced motion, clear feedback, and privacy are build constraints from the start.
